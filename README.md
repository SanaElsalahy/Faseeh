## Overview
Faseeh is a multi-agent AI platform for teaching Arabic to non-native speakers. Instead of one chatbot, several cooperating AI agents work together to support the learner.

This is my graduation project in Artificial Intelligence at Prince Mugrin University (UPM), Madinah.

## Why Faseeh
We noticed there is no platform for non-Arabic speakers to learn proper Arabic they can actually use, not just academic Arabic. Faseeh is built to fill that gap.
